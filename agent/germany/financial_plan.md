# 💰 Financial Plan — Germany Mission

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-04

---

## Financial Philosophy

**Money = Tool, not goal.**

Priority order:
1. Emergency fund
2. Investments
3. Relocation fund
4. Retirement
5. Experiences
6. Lifestyle upgrades

---

## Pre-Relocation (India, 2026-2028)

### Emergency Fund (Priority 1)
- Target: 6 months of Indian living expenses
- Keep in high-interest savings / liquid fund

### Investments (Priority 2)
- Start SIP in index funds / mutual funds
- Build investment habit early (small amounts consistent > large amounts late)

### Relocation Fund (Priority 3)
- Target: ₹3-5 lakhs minimum
- Covers: Visa fees, flight ticket, initial deposit, first month rent, living costs until first salary

### Lifestyle
- Avoid lifestyle inflation as income grows
- No unnecessary debt
- Keep expenses low to maximize savings rate

---

## Post-Relocation (Germany)

### First 6-12 Months
- Build German emergency fund (3-6 months expenses)
- Stabilize — understand cost of living, taxes, health insurance
- DO NOT buy luxury items (BMW, expensive gadgets) immediately
- Open German bank account (N26, DKB, ING)
- Get German health insurance (public first, evaluate private later)

### First 2-3 Years
- Max out investments (ETFs — MSCI World / Emerging Markets)
- Build long-term savings
- Avoid lifestyle inflation
- Start retirement planning (German pension + private)

---

## Car Goal (BMW)

**Approach:** Delayed gratification
- Do NOT buy immediately after relocating
- First build emergency fund + understand expenses
- Buy quality used BMW after financially comfortable (2-3 yrs post-relocation)
