**Last Updated:** 2026-07-04

## Mission Statement

Build a complete life in Germany while maximizing career growth, quality of life, safety, financial stability, and long-term happiness.

**Path:** Get hired directly from India by a German company → Relocate on work visa → Integrate → Settle permanently.

**Timeline:** ~5-8 years (2026-2034)

---

## Final Decision Rationale

Germany selected over US, Switzerland, Ireland, Netherlands, NZ because:

| Factor | Why Germany |
|--------|-------------|
| AI Eng opportunities | Strong AI/engineering ecosystem |
| Direct hiring from India | EU Blue Card + Indian talent pipeline |
| Work-life balance | 35-40hr weeks, 30+ leave days |
| Safety | Low crime, safe cities |
| Healthcare | Universal, high quality |
| Long-term settlement | PR after 33 months (Blue Card), citizenship after 5-8 yrs |
| Public transport | Excellent infrastructure |
| Worker protections | Strong labor laws |
| Economy | Stable, engineering-driven |
